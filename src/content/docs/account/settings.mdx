**Settings**, in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, opens a window over the page you are on, and the page under it stays as it was. On the left are a search and the sections, in groups; on the right is the section you choose, in rows: what each row is on its left, its value or button on its right. ×, **Esc** or a click beside the window closes it, and you are where you were.

- **Preferences**: **General**, **Account** and **Plan**.
- **Security**: **Keys** and **Devices**.
- **tTerm for Mac**, in the app: **This computer** and **Updates**.

<Screenshot name="settings" alt="The Settings window over a terminal's page: on the left Search and the sections, General, Account and Plan under Preferences, Keys and Devices under Security; on the right General: Appearance with Theme (System, Light, Dark) and Project colors (Code badge, Whole project), and Language with Interface language." />

Your name and email on top of the account menu open **Settings** → **Account**. Each section has an address of its own, such as app.tterm.net/settings/devices, so the email about a new sign-in leads straight to **Devices**. On a phone, Settings takes the whole screen: first the sections, then the one you tap, with the arrow back to them.

In tTerm for Mac before you sign in, Settings has only **General** and **Updates**, and what you choose there is saved on this computer.

## Search

**Search**, on top of the sections, keeps those that have every word you type, and lights their rows: `color` leaves **General**, with **Project colors** lit. Capitals and the marks over letters make no difference. **Esc** clears the search first, then closes the window.

<Screenshot name="settings-search" alt="Settings with theme typed in Search: on the left only General is left, and on the right its Theme row is lit." />

## General

- **Appearance**: the **Theme**, **System**, **Light** or **Dark**, and **Project colors**, **Code badge** or **Whole project**, more in [Colors](../../features/projects/#colors). They are saved to your account, on all your devices.
- **Language**: the **Interface language**, the same choice as **Language** in the account menu.

## Account

- **Photo**: **Add a photo**, or **Change photo** and **Remove photo**. Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP of up to 5 MB, place it in the circle in **Your photo** and press **Save photo**. It shows next to your name, in place of your initials; tTerm keeps a 256-pixel copy, without what the photo says about itself, such as where it was taken.
- **Name**: **Edit name**, type the new one and press **Save**.
- **Email**: the address you sign in with. To change it, write to [support@tterm.net](mailto:support@tterm.net).

**Deleting the account**: to delete your account, write to support@tterm.net from the address you sign in with. **Write to support**, beside **Delete account**, opens the window for it, as in [Write to support](../help/#write-to-support), and the answer comes to your email.

## Plan

Your plan, **Free**, with **Up to 50 machines and 24 open terminals**, and two meters of what you use now: **Machines** and **Open terminals**. Under it, faded, **Payment**: your **Payment method and invoices** will appear there with the paid plans. More in [Plan and limits](../plan/).

## Keys

Your [vault of SSH keys](../../security/keys/#settings--keys): whether it is open here, its password, your keys, and where the vault opens without the password.

## Devices

The browsers where you sign in with a quick code, and tTerm for Mac signed in with the browser: **Forget** one, or **Forget all devices…**. More in [Your devices](../devices/#your-devices).

## This computer

In tTerm for Mac, once you are signed in: the switch **Open from tTerm Web and your phone**, and the state of this Mac's link to tTerm Web. More in [A Mac with tTerm for Mac](../../machines/remove/#a-mac-with-tterm-for-mac).

## Updates

In tTerm for Mac: **Update by itself** or **Ask me first**, and **Check now**. **Check for Updates…** in tTerm's menu opens this section too. More in [Updates](../../local/mac/#updates) of the Mac version.
