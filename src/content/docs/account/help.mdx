The account at the bottom of the sidebar opens a menu with **Get help**:

- **Documentation** opens these pages, in the language tTerm speaks.
- **Write to support** opens a window to write to us.

<Screenshot name="help-menu" alt="The account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, with Get help pointed at and its list beside it: Documentation and Write to support." />

## Write to support

Say what happened in your own words under **What happened**, and press **Send** or **⌘Enter** (**Ctrl+Enter** on Windows and Linux). The answer comes to the email of your account, and a copy of your message is there at once, so you have it too.

**Attach technical details** is ticked at first. Then one line goes with the message, and right under the tick it shows exactly as it goes: the version of tTerm Web, your browser and its system, your plan and how many machines you have – **tTerm Web 0.1.25 · Chrome 141, macOS · Free plan · 3 machines**, say. Never your commands, what they printed or your notes. Untick it to send the message alone.

<Screenshot name="write-support" alt="Write to support: We will answer at anna@example.com; under What happened, a message that web-01's log won't open for 30 October; Attach technical details ticked, with the line that goes: tTerm Web 0.1.25 · Chrome 141, macOS · Free plan · 3 machines; a link to the documentation; Cancel and Send." />

**Message sent** then says where the answer will come.

In tTerm for Mac before you sign in, the window asks for **Your email**, and the answer comes there. No copy goes to it, as nobody has checked that address.

You can send five messages an hour; before signing in, three an hour from one network. If a message does not go, the window says so and gives the address to write to yourself: [support@tterm.net](mailto:support@tterm.net).

## About us

**About us**, in the same menu, leads to the **tterm.net website**, our **Telegram channel** and [**What's new**](../../whats-new/web/): what changed in each version of tTerm Web, or of tTerm for Mac in the app.
