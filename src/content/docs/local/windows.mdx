The local terminal for Windows is in the works. When it is out, we will say so in our [channel](https://t.me/tTermBlog) and on the [blog](https://tterm.net/blog/).

## Right now

- **The cloud terminal** works in any browser on Windows: open [app.tterm.net](https://app.tterm.net), and all your servers and computers are at hand.
- A Windows computer cannot be added as a machine yet. Linux servers and computers on macOS or Linux can: a [server](../../machines/servers/), a [computer](../../machines/computers/).
