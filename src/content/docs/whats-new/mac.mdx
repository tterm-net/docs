What each version of tTerm for Mac changed for you, the newest first. Where a version brought a new page of tTerm Web into the window, its entry links to [what changed there](../web/), and the technical details are in the [releases on GitHub](https://github.com/tterm-net/tterm-app/releases).

## 0.7.1

1 November 2026

- [Move to cloud](../../local/move-to-cloud/) takes each server the way you reached it: with the user and port you typed in `ssh` in tTerm's terminals (`ssh root@host`, `-l`, `-p`), over those in `~/.ssh/config`.
- **Move to cloud** is now at the bottom of the account menu, right above **Sign out**: the page [0.1.19](../web/#0119).

## 0.7.0

1 November 2026

- [Move to cloud](../../local/move-to-cloud/) in the account menu: the servers this Mac knows, from `~/.ssh/config`, `known_hosts` and the `ssh` you ran in tTerm, go to your account and open from anywhere. Each goes the way you choose, **My key**, **tTerm's pass** or **Through this Mac**, and nothing moves until you press **Move**.
- After a successful `ssh` in a terminal of this Mac, a strip offers to keep that server in tTerm: **Save my key** or **Let tTerm in**.
- Your keys go to a [vault](../../security/keys/) that only your devices open; tTerm cannot. On this Mac it opens with Touch ID or your Mac's password and stays open while tTerm runs: this Mac then signs your servers in, also when you open them from tTerm Web or your phone.
- A new browser that asks for your keys is approved on this Mac with **Allow with Touch ID**, if it shows the same code.
- The page [0.1.17](../web/#0117): **Settings** → **Keys**, and servers that open with your key or through your Mac.

## 0.6.0

31 October 2026

- The first version on tterm.net, [the local terminal](../../local/mac/): the shells of this Mac as blocks, with tTerm's input line, and notes kept on this Mac, all with no account.
- **Sign in with your browser** brings your servers, projects and notes into the same window, and this Mac becomes a machine in Personal. **⌘K** searches this Mac's commands and the cloud's as one list.
- In **Settings** → **This computer**, the switch **Open from tTerm Web and your phone** lets tTerm Web and your phone open terminals on this Mac while tTerm runs. With its window closed, tTerm stays in the menu bar.
- tTerm updates itself from tTerm's releases: **Update by itself** or **Ask me first**, in **Settings** → **Updates**.
- tTerm speaks twelve languages, its menus and the menu bar too: **Language** in the account menu. Until you choose, it speaks your system's language if it is one of the twelve, and English otherwise. The page [0.1.16](../web/#0116).
